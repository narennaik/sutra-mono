Sutra Mono is a monospaced typeface for code, drawn as a single thread. Every letter is one centre-line traced with a round pen, so terminals end in the same soft curve, nothing has a sharp corner, and weight is a single parameter. Bowls follow a superellipse that sits between a circle and a square, which keeps columns of text calm and open.

Characters that are easily confused in code are clearly distinct: a dotted zero, a barred capital I, a tailed lowercase l and a flagged one. Mathematical and functional-programming symbols such as ∘ → ⇒ ↦ λ ∀ ∃ ≡ ≅ ⊗ ⊕ are drawn with the same pen and sit on the operator axis.

The family comes in Light, Regular and Bold and covers the GF Latin Core character set. To contribute, see github.com/narennaik/sutra-mono.
